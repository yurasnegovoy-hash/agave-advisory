What we cover

Barrel & Aging Advisory

For distilleries and brands working on reposado, añejo and extra añejo expressions, and for anyone who needs barrels delivered to a distillery in Mexico

01

Barrel selection

American white oak ex-bourbon barrels, 53 gal / 200 L, from leading Kentucky and Tennessee producers. French oak on request

02

Quality of every barrel

Freshly dumped, used once, checked for leaks and physical damage. Phytosanitary certificates provided

03

Aging recommendations

How long and in which wood to age: reposado from 2 months, añejo from 1 year, extra añejo from 3 years

04

Flavour profile

How barrel and time shape the character: vanilla, roasted coffee, chocolate, nuts, coconut

05

Storage conditions

Barrels rest in rooms with controlled humidity and no light. We advise on the setup

06

Delivery to the distillery

From the cooperage to your distillery in Mexico. Container quantities and schedule
