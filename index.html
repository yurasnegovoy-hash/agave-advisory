How we work
How tequila is made

We stand at every stage
from the field to the bottle

Real footage from the fields and distilleries of Los Altos de Jalisco, where we work every day

01

Highland fields

Agave grows for 6 to 10 years at 2000 metres

Blue Weber agave of Los Altos grows slowly in red volcanic soil. Bigger piñas, more sugar, more aroma

02

Harvest

Jimadores cut the piña by hand with the coa

Only ripe plants are harvested. A highland piña can weigh up to 110 kg

03

Cooking

12 hours at 110 °C

Slow cooking turns the starches of the piña into sugars and sets the foundation of flavour

04

Milling

Agua miel and bagazo

The cooked piñas are crushed in the mill. The sweet juice, agua miel, goes to fermentation. The fibre, bagazo, returns to the fields as fertiliser

05

Fermentation

Up to 72 hours on the distillery's own yeast

Yeast and time give each tequila its aroma: citrus, flowers and fruit from the agave, vanilla from the process. We follow every tank with our partner distillery

06

Distillation

Twice, in copper

The first run removes heads and tails, the second sets the strength. Copper stills at our partner distillery are over a century old

07

Filtration

Clean, not stripped

Filtration removes what does not belong in the glass and keeps the agave

08

Aging

American and French oak

Reposado from 2 months, añejo from 1 year, extra añejo from 3. Barrels rest in rooms with controlled humidity

09

Bottling

Under our supervision

We stand at the line: packaging check, quality inspection, photo and video report, final approval before shipment

10

Delivery

To your market

Export documents, forwarder, port and door-to-door delivery on EXW, FOB, CIF or other terms

Why it matters

Know where
your tequila comes from

Many brands buy bulk tequila from different distilleries with no single quality standard. The taste of tequila depends on every stage: the agave, the cooking, the yeast, fermentation, distillation and filtration

Tequila may only be made in five Mexican states, and the highlands of Los Altos give the sweetest agave. We work with distilleries there, where we know the origin of the agave and how the liquid was made. That is how quality stays the same from batch to batch
