Why an agency

What a distillery
will not do for you

A distillery makes tequila. It does not choose your bottle supplier, file your trademark, check your labels with the regulator, stand at your bottling line or book your forwarder. When you are in another country, someone has to

  • We choose the distillery that fits your product, not the one that answers first
  • We know who makes good glass, closures and packaging in Mexico, and at what price
  • We stand at the line when your tequila is bottled and send you photo and video reports
  • We organise export on EXW, FOB, CIF or any terms your market needs
How we choose a distillery

Not every tequila
is made the same way

In Mexico there are around 200 certified producers and over 3000 registered tequila brands. The difference between them is in the process

01

Agave

Where it grew, how old it is, how much sugar it holds. Highland agave of Los Altos is larger and sweeter

02

Cooking and yeast

Slow cooking and the yeast strain decide the aromas: citrus, fruit, flowers, vanilla

03

Distillation

Copper or steel, how heads and tails are cut, how long the spirit is filtered

04

Consistency

Whether batch 10 tastes like batch 1. We look for distilleries where it does
